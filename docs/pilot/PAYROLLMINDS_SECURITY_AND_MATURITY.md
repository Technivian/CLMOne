# PayrollMinds Security and Maturity Statement

CLM One is suitable for a **controlled, synthetic design-partner demo**. It is
not represented as production-ready for sensitive client, employee, or payroll
data.

The demonstration is local-only and resettable. Its seed command refuses a
deployed platform, uses fictional identities and `.example` addresses, and
does not connect to an e-sign, payroll, HRIS, identity, or AI provider. AI is
disabled; no document text is sent to a model provider.

The application has tenant-scoping, authentication, audit, and focused
workflow tests that support the demo's bounded claims. Those controls do not
establish complete object-level authorization, Ethical-Wall enforcement,
analytics permission enforcement, AI-redaction enforcement, data residency,
SIEM integration, penetration-test coverage, or production operational
readiness.

No real PayrollMinds, customer, employee, payroll, bank, tax, or personal data
may be entered during this tranche. Any move beyond synthetic rehearsal needs
a separate security, privacy, data-classification, authorization, operational,
and release assessment under the active governance process.
