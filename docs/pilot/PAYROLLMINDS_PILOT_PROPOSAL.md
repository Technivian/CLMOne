# PayrollMinds Controlled Design-Partner Pilot Proposal

## Purpose

Validate whether CLM One gives PayrollMinds stakeholders a clearer operating
view of a global payroll transformation agreement set: request, review,
approval, signature preparation, record, and obligations.

## Initial scope

One controlled workspace; the fictional NDA, MSA, SOW, and DPA journey; Legal,
Finance, Privacy, and Procurement viewpoints; contract repository; activity;
and obligations/renewal visibility. The first sessions use synthetic data only.

## Working model

- 4–6 named design-partner participants
- weekly 45-minute walkthrough and feedback session
- resettable synthetic rehearsal before every session
- issue log focused on clarity, workflow fit, and demo-blocking defects
- no real client, employee, payroll, bank, tax, or personal data

## Success measures

Participants can explain the agreement set, locate the Finance condition and
Privacy risks, identify the next owner, find the resulting record, and see the
important post-signature dates without facilitator intervention.

## Explicit exclusions

This is not production deployment, legal advice, a live e-sign integration,
or authorization for real payroll data. AI is disabled. Ethical-Wall,
enterprise analytics, AI-redaction enforcement, data residency, and full
security certification are not represented as complete.

## Decision at the end of the pilot

Agree whether to continue with a separately scoped pilot hardening and data
assessment plan, or stop without importing any real data.
