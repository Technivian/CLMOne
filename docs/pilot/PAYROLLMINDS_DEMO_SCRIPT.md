# PayrollMinds Demo Script

**Duration:** 15 minutes plus questions. Use only the reset synthetic workspace
and its server-enforced read-only presenter route.

1. **Set the boundary (1 minute).** State that this is a controlled
   design-partner demonstration using fictional data and that AI, production
   controls, and real signature delivery are not in scope. The visible records
   are a synthetic operational snapshot, not a claim of a live workflow.
2. **Start a request (2 minutes).** Open New Contract and frame the MSA/SOW
   route as the entry point for the Global Payroll Transformation Engagement.
3. **Show the intake (2 minutes).** Open the Global Payroll Transformation SOW
   and point out EUR 240,000, the four countries, the two vendors, privacy
   flags, and DPA requirement.
4. **Show the agreement set (2 minutes).** In Contracts, show the fictional
   NDA, MSA, SOW, and DPA. Open the SOW and its linked MSA.
5. **Show review ownership (3 minutes).** Open My Work/Approvals: Legal is
   complete, Finance is pending because the value exceeds EUR 100,000, and
   Privacy owns the open DPA review. Explain the displayed queue as evidence
   of ownership; no decision controls are available in presenter mode.
6. **Show the privacy decision context (2 minutes).** Open the DPA review
   pack. Explain the three explicit, human-owned risk positions: breach
   timing, audit rights, and liability alignment. Do not say that AI found
   them.
7. **Show signature and record (2 minutes).** Show the SOW's prepared
   synthetic signature request and the executed MSA evidence, then return to
   the contract record and its contract-scoped activity history. Do not open
   the global audit ledger or use any signature action.
8. **Operate after signature (1 minute).** Open Obligations and show the
   MSA renewal-and-notice decision, subprocessor review, implementation
   milestone, pricing indexation, and NDA-expiry deadlines. Do not promise
   notice delivery or mark any obligation complete.

Close with the pilot proposition: one controlled workspace, synthetic-first
onboarding, a narrow agreement and review scope, weekly feedback, and no live
client/payroll data until separately assessed.
