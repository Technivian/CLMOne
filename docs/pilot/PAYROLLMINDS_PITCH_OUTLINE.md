# PayrollMinds Pitch Outline

1. **The problem:** global payroll transformations create connected MSA, SOW,
   DPA, vendor, approval, and renewal work that is hard to see in one place.
2. **The proposition:** CLM One gives the legal and operating teams one
   governed view of a focused contract journey.
3. **The engagement:** fictional Global Payroll Transformation Engagement;
   four countries, two vendors, one EUR 240,000 implementation SOW.
4. **Intake and structure:** capture value, geography, vendors, privacy risk,
   and required NDA/MSA/SOW/DPA paper at the start.
5. **Controlled review:** Legal, Finance, and Privacy each see explicit work;
   Finance is conditionally required for the EUR 100,000 threshold.
6. **From paper to record:** versioned documents, prepared signature evidence,
   contract record, and visible activity history.
7. **Operate the commitment:** milestones, renewal decision, subprocessor
   review, indexation, and notice dates become owned work.
8. **Pilot together:** controlled design-partner scope, synthetic-first
   rehearsal, weekly feedback, and an explicit non-production maturity boundary.
